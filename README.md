## 3. Research Question
**How do cultural, religious, and moral-value orientations shape the perception of AI risks and benefits, and how these perceptions influence youth acceptance of AI chatbots across different socio-economic contexts?**

## 4. Hypothesis and Methods Used

### Hypotheses
1. Cultural Risk Priorities: Youth in advanced economies prioritize privacy, surveillance, and bias; emerging economies prioritize job loss and economic disruption.
2. Religious Influence: Higher religiosity predicts stronger emotional concern toward AI.
3. Moral Foundations: Individualizing foundations increase bias sensitivity; Binding foundations reduce it.
4. Youth Chatbot Adoption: Utility increases trust; risk reduces trust. Cultural context moderates relationships.

### Methods Used
- Mixed-method research design combining cross-national indices, surveys, moral foundations assessment, and global student data.

#### H1 – Risk Priorities Across Cultures
- Method: Quantitative cross-country comparison
- Data Source: Ipsos AI Monitor 2024–2025

#### H2 – Religiosity & Emotional Responses
- Method: Survey data analysis (N = 1,050)
- Measures: Religiosity + emotional reactions

#### H3 – Moral Foundations & Bias Sensitivity
- Method: MFQ survey + AI scenarios. (should be MFQ based form + AI scenarios)


#### H4 – Youth Chatbot Acceptance
- Method: Survey-based quantitative analysis
- Data Source: Global ChatGPT Student Survey
- Measures: Utility, Risk, Trust, Adoption Intention, Culture
